Antes naquele lugar eu. Nós aqui.

My work "Antes naquele lugar eu. Nós aqui." (2011) is part of the collection of the Museum of Contemporary Art of Rio Grande do Sul and is on view in the exhibition Acervo em Foco: Paisagem Sonora, on the 5th floor of the Casa de Cultura Mario Quintana, until 20 November 2026.

The work consists of four obelisks, each holding a soundscape, and a control unit fitted with a joystick. When the viewer takes on the role of interactor, moving the joystick releases the soundscapes held in the obelisks and lets them be combined according to the movements of whoever operates the system — an interaction logic inspired by arcade games, which raises the question of whether this sonic gameplay, when given rhythm, can become music, composing the soundscape of the moment live.

I recorded the four soundscapes in Pelotas in 2011, the year the city turned two hundred — the proposal of a synaesthetic image, a sound that evokes and replaces the sight of a specific place at a specific time. The great flood that struck Rio Grande do Sul in 2024 damaged the work, and that damage brought the unexpected opportunity to revisit it: in the repair, carried out in 2026, the original sound technology was replaced with very similar electronics from the same historical period, following the principle of reusing discarded objects that runs through all my work.
