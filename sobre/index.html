About

André Barbachan is a visual artist, musician, researcher and teacher, born in Herval (RS, Brazil) and based in Pelotas. He holds a PhD in Visual Arts from UFRGS and a master's and bachelor's degree in the same field from UFPel, and creates interactive installations and objects that combine sound, electronics and the reuse of discarded materials.

His work "Antes naquele lugar eu. Nós aqui." (2011) is part of the collection of the Museum of Contemporary Art of Rio Grande do Sul. He is also a guitarist, co-founder of the band Canastra Suja, and founder of Ateliê MAKER.

In 2026 he released Milonga Lo-fi, an album that blends traditional gaucho milonga with contemporary lo-fi, recorded outdoors in Herval in the winter of 2022 — nine tracks in which the wind, the lapwings and the silence of the border shape the texture alongside the guitar. Listen to the album.
